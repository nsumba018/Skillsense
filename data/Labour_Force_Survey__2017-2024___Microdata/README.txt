Labour Force Survey (2017-2024): Microdata
==========================================

Summary: The Labour Force Survey (LFS) programme conducted by the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda provides official labour market statistics for Rwanda. The survey measures employment, unemployment, labour force participation, underemployment, and other work-related characteristics. It supports national planning and monitoring of NST2, SDGs, and Vision 2050. The programme started in 2016, was bi-annual until 2018, and became quarterly from 2019 onwards.

Geographic Coverage: National coverage across Rwanda, with indicators disaggregated by district and residential area (depending on the year).

Time Period: 2017–2024, covering bi-annual rounds (2017–2018) and quarterly rounds (2019–2024).

Frequency: Bi-annual (2017–2018), Quarterly (2019–2024), with annual estimates produced from combined rounds.

Population/Units: Individuals aged 16+ living in private households in Rwanda. Institutional populations and persons in seasonal or work-site dwellings are excluded.

Key Variables: Employment status, unemployment, labour force participation, underemployment, job characteristics, hours worked, income from employment, own-use production, and household characteristics.

Purpose: To provide reliable labour statistics for policy formulation, monitoring labour market trends, and supporting national development planning.

Data Quality Notes: The LFS uses a two-stage stratified sampling design with weighting and calibration to population projections. Data are collected using CAPI, with generally high response rates. Methodological changes occurred during the transition to quarterly surveys.
