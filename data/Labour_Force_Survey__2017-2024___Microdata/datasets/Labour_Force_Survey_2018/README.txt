Labour Force Survey 2018
========================

Summary: This dataset contains annual labour force statistics for Rwanda, compiled from the 2018 Labour Force Survey conducted by the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda. The 2018 survey was implemented during a transition period from a bi-annual to a quarterly survey design, with data collected in February and August 2018. The two survey rounds were combined to produce annual estimates. The survey captures key labour market indicators including employment status, unemployment, labour force participation, time-related underemployment, own-use production work, and household characteristics. The reference population includes individuals aged 16 years and above living in private households in Rwanda.

Geographic Coverage: National coverage across Rwanda, with labour indicators at national level.

Time Period: 2018, covering two survey rounds conducted in February 2018 and August 2018.

Frequency: Bi-annual (two data collection rounds per year, aggregated into an annual dataset).

Population/Units: Individuals aged 16 years and above living in private households in Rwanda. Institutional populations (e.g., hostels, health facilities, correctional institutions) and persons living in seasonal dwellings or at work sites are excluded.

Key Variables: Employment status, unemployment status, labour force participation, time-related underemployment, job characteristics (main and secondary employment), multiple jobholding, hours worked, income-related variables, own-use production work, subsistence production, and household characteristics.

Purpose: The dataset provides official labour market statistics for monitoring employment trends, labour force participation, and socio-economic conditions in Rwanda to support national planning and policy formulation.

Data Quality Notes: The survey uses a two-stage stratified sampling design with probability proportional to size selection and systematic household sampling within enumeration areas. Weighting includes design weights, non-response adjustments, and calibration to population projections. The 2018 survey achieved a response rate of 98.7%. Data collection was conducted using face-to-face interviews and CAPI tools, with strong field supervision and consistency checks. Data may still be subject to sampling and non-sampling errors.
