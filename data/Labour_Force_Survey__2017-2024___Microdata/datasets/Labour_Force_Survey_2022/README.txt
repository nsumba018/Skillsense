Labour Force Survey 2022
========================

Summary: This dataset contains annual labour force statistics for Rwanda, compiled from the 2022 Labour Force Survey conducted across four quarters (February, May, August, and November 2022) by the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda. The four survey rounds were combined to produce annual estimates at national and district levels where applicable. Data were collected using Computer-Assisted Personal Interviews (CAPI) in selected households. The survey captures key labour market indicators including employment status, unemployment, labour force participation, income from employment, migrant workers, and persons with disabilities. The reference population includes individuals aged 16 years and above living in private households in Rwanda.

Geographic Coverage: National coverage across Rwanda, with key labour indicators disaggregated at national, district, and residential area levels.

Time Period: 2022, covering four survey rounds conducted in February, May, August, and November 2022.

Frequency: Quarterly (four data collection rounds per year, aggregated into an annual dataset).

Population/Units: Individuals aged 16 years and above living in private households in Rwanda. Institutional populations (e.g., hostels, health facilities, correctional institutions) and persons living in seasonal dwellings or at work sites are excluded.

Key Variables: Employment status, unemployment status, labour force participation, time-related underemployment, job characteristics (main and secondary employment), income from employment, migrant workers, workers with disabilities, and household characteristics.

Purpose: The dataset provides official labour market statistics to support monitoring of employment trends, labour force participation, and socio-economic conditions in Rwanda for evidence-based planning and policy formulation.

Data Quality Notes: The survey applies a two-stage stratified sampling design with weighting adjustments for design, non-response, and calibration to population projections. The response rate for the 2022 survey was 90.0%. Data may be affected by sampling and non-sampling errors, and results may vary due to seasonal labour fluctuations.
