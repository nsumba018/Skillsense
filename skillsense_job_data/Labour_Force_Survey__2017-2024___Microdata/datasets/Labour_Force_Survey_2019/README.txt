Labour Force Survey 2019
========================

Summary: This dataset contains annual labour force statistics for Rwanda, compiled from the 2019 Labour Force Survey conducted across four quarters (February, May, August, and November 2019) by the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda. The survey design transitioned in 2019 from bi-annual to quarterly data collection to improve labour market monitoring. The four survey rounds were combined to produce annual estimates at national and district levels where applicable. The survey captures key labour market indicators including employment status, unemployment, labour force participation, income from employment, own-use production work, labour underutilization, and household characteristics. The reference population includes individuals aged 16 years and above living in private households in Rwanda.

Geographic Coverage: National coverage across Rwanda, with labour indicators disaggregated at national, district, and residential area levels.

Time Period: 2019, covering four survey rounds conducted in February, May, August, and November 2019.

Frequency: Quarterly (four data collection rounds per year, aggregated into an annual dataset).

Population/Units: Individuals aged 16 years and above living in private households in Rwanda. Institutional populations (e.g., hostels, health facilities, correctional institutions) and persons living in seasonal dwellings or at work sites are excluded.

Key Variables: Employment status, unemployment status, labour force participation, time-related underemployment, job characteristics (main and secondary employment), multiple jobholding, hours worked, income from employment, own-use production work, subsistence production, housing characteristics, and demographic variables.

Purpose: The dataset provides official labour market statistics for monitoring employment trends, labour force participation, and socio-economic conditions in Rwanda to support evidence-based planning and policy formulation.

Data Quality Notes: The survey uses a two-stage stratified sampling design with probability proportional to size selection and household sampling within enumeration areas. The 2019 survey achieved a response rate of 98.6%. Data collection was conducted using CAPI with strong quality control procedures, including field supervision, consistency checks, and post-collection editing using CsPro and Stata. Data may still be subject to sampling and non-sampling errors.
