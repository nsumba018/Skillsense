Labour Force Survey 2023
========================

Summary: This dataset contains annual labour force statistics for Rwanda, compiled from the 2023 Labour Force Survey conducted across four quarters (February, May, August, and November 2023) by the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda. Data were collected using Computer-Assisted Personal Interviews (CAPI) through face-to-face interviews in selected households. The survey covers key labour market indicators including employment status, unemployment, labour force participation, income from employment, migrant workers, and persons with disabilities. The reference population includes individuals aged 16 years and above living in private households in Rwanda.

Geographic Coverage: National coverage across Rwanda, with key labour indicators disaggregated at national, district, and residential area levels.

Time Period: 2023, covering four survey rounds conducted in February, May, August, and November 2023.

Frequency: Quarterly (four data collection rounds per year, aggregated into an annual dataset).

Population/Units: Individuals aged 16 years and above living in private households in Rwanda. Institutional populations (e.g., hostels, health facilities, correctional institutions) and persons living in seasonal dwellings or work sites are excluded.

Key Variables: Employment status, unemployment status, labour force participation, time-related underemployment, job characteristics (main and secondary employment), past employment, own-use production, subsistence production, income from employment, and household characteristics.

Purpose: The dataset is designed to provide official labour market statistics for monitoring employment trends, labour force participation, and socio-economic conditions in Rwanda to support national planning and policy formulation.

Data Quality Notes: The survey uses a two-stage stratified sampling design with weighting adjustments for design, non-response, and population calibration. The response rate for the 2023 survey was 96.0%. Data are subject to sampling and non-sampling errors, and results may vary slightly due to seasonal labour fluctuations.
