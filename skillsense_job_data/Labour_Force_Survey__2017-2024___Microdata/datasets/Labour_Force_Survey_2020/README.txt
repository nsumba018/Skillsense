Labour Force Survey 2020
========================

Summary: This dataset contains annual labour force statistics for Rwanda, compiled from the 2020 Labour Force Survey conducted across four quarters (February, May, August, and November 2020) by the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda. The four survey rounds were combined to produce annual estimates at national and district levels where applicable. The May 2020 round was conducted on a reduced subsample due to COVID-19 constraints and included only households with valid telephone contacts. Data collection for May and November 2020 was conducted using Computer-Assisted Personal Interviews (CAPI) with partial online administration. The survey captures key labour market indicators including employment status, unemployment, labour force participation, income from employment, migrant workers, and persons with disabilities. The reference population includes individuals aged 16 years and above living in private households in Rwanda.

Geographic Coverage: National coverage across Rwanda, with labour indicators disaggregated at national, district, and residential area levels.

Time Period: 2020, covering four survey rounds conducted in February, May, August, and November 2020.

Frequency: Quarterly (four data collection rounds per year, aggregated into an annual dataset).

Population/Units: Individuals aged 16 years and above living in private households in Rwanda. Institutional populations (e.g., hostels, health facilities, correctional institutions) and persons living in seasonal dwellings or at work sites are excluded.

Key Variables: Employment status, unemployment status, labour force participation, time-related underemployment, job characteristics (main and secondary employment), income from employment, migrant workers, workers with disabilities, and household characteristics.

Purpose: The dataset provides official labour market statistics to support monitoring of employment trends, labour force participation, and socio-economic conditions in Rwanda for evidence-based planning and policy formulation.

Data Quality Notes: The survey uses a two-stage stratified sampling design with probability proportional to size selection, household listing, and subsample adjustments due to COVID-19 disruptions. Weighting includes design weights, non-response adjustments, and calibration to population projections. The response rate for the 2020 survey was 94.6%. Data quality may be affected by methodological changes in the May 2020 round and pandemic-related data collection constraints.
