Labour Force Survey 2017
========================

Summary: This dataset contains annual labour force statistics for Rwanda, compiled from the 2017 Labour Force Survey conducted by the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda. The survey was implemented as part of the early LFS programme covering key labour market indicators including employment status, unemployment, labour force participation, time-related underemployment, own-use production work, and household characteristics. The reference population includes individuals aged 16 years and above living in private households in Rwanda.

Geographic Coverage: National coverage across Rwanda, with labour indicators reported at national level.

Time Period: 2017, covering the Labour Force Survey round conducted between August and October 2017.

Frequency: Annual (single main survey round used for annual estimates).

Population/Units: Individuals aged 16 years and above living in private households in Rwanda. Institutional populations (e.g., hostels, health facilities, correctional institutions) and persons living in seasonal dwellings or at work sites are excluded.

Key Variables: Employment status, unemployment status, labour force participation, time-related underemployment, job characteristics (main and secondary employment), multiple jobholding, hours worked, income-related variables, own-use production work, subsistence production, and household characteristics.

Purpose: The dataset provides official labour market statistics to support monitoring of employment trends, labour force participation, and socio-economic conditions in Rwanda for evidence-based planning and policy formulation.

Data Quality Notes: The survey uses a two-stage stratified sampling design with probability proportional to size selection and systematic household sampling within enumeration areas. Weighting includes design weights, non-response adjustments, and calibration to population projections. The 2017 survey achieved a response rate of 98.4%. Data collection was conducted face-to-face using structured questionnaires translated into Kinyarwanda and English, with strong field supervision and consistency checks. Data may still be subject to sampling and non-sampling errors.
