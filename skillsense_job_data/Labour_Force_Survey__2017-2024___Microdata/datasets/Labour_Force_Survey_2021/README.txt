Labour Force Survey 2021
========================

Summary: This dataset contains annual labour force statistics for Rwanda, compiled from the 2021 Labour Force Survey conducted across four quarters (February, May, August, and November 2021) by the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda. The four survey rounds were combined to produce annual estimates at national and district levels where applicable. Data were collected using Computer-Assisted Personal Interviews (CAPI) in selected households. The survey captures key labour market indicators including employment status, unemployment, labour force participation, income from employment, migrant workers, and persons with disabilities. The reference population includes individuals aged 16 years and above living in private households in Rwanda.

Geographic Coverage: National coverage across Rwanda, with labour indicators disaggregated at national, district, and residential area levels.

Time Period: 2021, covering four survey rounds conducted in February, May, August, and November 2021.

Frequency: Quarterly (four data collection rounds per year, aggregated into an annual dataset).

Population/Units: Individuals aged 16 years and above living in private households in Rwanda. Institutional populations (e.g., hostels, health facilities, correctional institutions) and persons living in seasonal dwellings or at work sites are excluded.

Key Variables: Employment status, unemployment status, labour force participation, time-related underemployment, job characteristics (main and secondary employment), income from employment, migrant workers, workers with disabilities, household characteristics, sampling weights, and survey design variables.

Purpose: The dataset provides official labour market statistics to support monitoring of employment trends, labour force participation, and socio-economic conditions in Rwanda for evidence-based planning and policy formulation.

Data Quality Notes: The survey uses a two-stage stratified sampling design with probability proportional to size selection, household listing, and rotation sampling. Weighting adjustments include design weights, non-response adjustments, and calibration to population projections. The response rate and precision are supported by a large sample design optimized for unemployment estimation. Data may be affected by sampling and non-sampling errors, and results may vary due to seasonal labour fluctuations.
